# IMAGE SEO & PHOTOGRAPHY OPTIMIZATION GUIDE — LOVEY'S BAKERY & PIZZA CORNERS

Visual search (Google Images, Google Lens, and Google Business Profile photos) is one of the highest-converting channels for custom celebration cakes and pizzas in Renukoot.

---

## 1. Descriptive Alt Text Standard
Every product, category, and gallery image supports a dedicated **Descriptive Image Alt Text (`imageAlt`)** field in the Admin Dashboard (`/admin/products` and `/admin/media`).

**Formula**:
`[Product / Cake Name] — [Weight / Key Feature, e.g. 100% Eggless] at Lovey's Bakery & Pizza Corners, Renukoot`

**Examples**:
- *Good*: `Signature Belgian Chocolate Truffle Gateau — 100% eggless celebration cake at Lovey's Bakery & Pizza Corners, Renukoot`
- *Good*: `Royal Rasmalai Pistachio Fusion Cake with saffron milk soak and rose petals in Renukoot`
- *Avoid*: `IMG_2049.jpg`, `cake1.png`, or keyword stuffing (`best cake shop renukoot cheap cake renukoot birthday cake`).

---

## 2. Image Sitemap Integration (`xmlns:image`)
- Every active category, verified product, and visible gallery photograph is automatically included in `/sitemap.xml` with `<image:image>`, `<image:loc>`, `<image:title>`, and `<image:caption>` tags.
- Ensure all uploaded product images use clear, high-contrast lighting without watermarks or third-party stock photos.

---

## 3. Recommended Dimensions & Formats
- **Product & Cake Cards**: `1200 × 900 px` (`4:3` aspect ratio), WebP or progressive JPEG (`< 180 KB`).
- **Open Graph / Social Share Banner**: `1200 × 630 px` (`1.91:1` aspect ratio).
- **Square Gallery / Instagram Showcase**: `1080 × 1080 px` (`1:1` aspect ratio).
